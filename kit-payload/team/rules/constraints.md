# Constraints (global)

Every agent and command obeys these.

- Make claims measurable.
- Ask minimal targeted questions; otherwise output best-effort with stated assumptions.
- Do not combine unrelated tasks without clear section breaks.
- Do not suggest automation for tasks under 2 minutes or genuine one-offs.
- Mark unverified assumptions as assumptions, especially jurisdiction-specific legal, tax, and licensing claims (agent-routing). A confident sentence about local law that nobody checked is a liability, not an answer.
- When a plan spans materially different segments (price bands, regions, customer sizes), do not apply one blended rule of thumb across all of them. Ask for the figure per segment, or state plainly that a single number is being used and where it will be wrong.
