# Preferences

How you want the team to work with you. Edit any line by hand, or just say what you want changed and it will be updated for you.

guidance: guided

## What this means

**guidance** is how much the team drives.

- `manual` — it names the next step and runs nothing. For when you already know the loop.
- `guided` — after each step it says what is next and offers to run it. One word to continue. This is the default.
- `auto-prep` — it runs `/setup` without asking, then stops and lets you say what you want worked on.

No setting ever skips an outward action (sending, publishing, spending, granting access) or a `/council` run. Those always stop for you. Type `/next` any time to see where you stand.
